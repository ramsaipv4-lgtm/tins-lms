## Decision
**Problem:** Store graded attempts, keep every mark and correction, and let a learner appeal with evidence for the trainer.
**Options considered:** overwrite a score field; score field plus change log; hash-chained ledger entries with the score copied onto the attempt.
**Choice:** ledger entries (first sign-off, then corrections with `corrects`), attempt keeps a copy of the latest score.
**Why:** P-10 and AC-21 require the original to stay; the chain makes edits detectable.
