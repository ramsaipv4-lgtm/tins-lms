# Instructor script — Recovery words and crypto-shredding

1. (3 min) Ask: how do you make data erasable when copies exist everywhere? Take answers.
2. (5 min) Show the byte to word mapping; draw a 256-slot list.
3. (5 min) Walk through wrapPersonKey and shred in keys.ts.
4. (5 min) Show the word-list miscount failure; have learners predict the output.
5. (5 min) Check yourself questions; collect answers aloud.
