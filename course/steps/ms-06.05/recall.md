# Recall — Attempts, grade ledger, appeals and integrity log

## Exercise 1 — Trace a correction (5 min)
**What to do:** Grade an attempt 5, then 7. List the ledger.
**The answer (check after):** Two entries; the second has corrects 0; both stay.

## Cards
**Q:** What does a grade correction add to the ledger?
**A:** A new entry whose corrects names the earlier seq; the original is kept.

**Q:** How long is the appeal window and from when?
**A:** 7 days from when the grade was first published.

**Q:** What is in the evidence pack?
**A:** Seed, mode, events, rubric rows and the unread-confirmation count.

**Q:** Who gets 403 on grade sign-off?
**A:** Learners and substitutes; trainers and admins pass.
