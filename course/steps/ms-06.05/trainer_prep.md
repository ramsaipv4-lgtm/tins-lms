# Trainer prep — Attempts, grade ledger, appeals and integrity log

## Before you start (prerequisites)
Learners know the ledger idea from ms-06.01 and have the repo running.

## 45-minute self-study path
Read the lesson, run the grading tests, then do the reinforcement activity.

## Worked example → faded example
Worked: grade 5 then correct to 7. Faded: learners add the appeal decision route.

## Top misconceptions
- A correction edits the old entry (it appends a new one).
- The appeal window starts at submission (it starts at publication).

## Questions students will ask (with answers)
**Why not store only the score?** Because history and tamper evidence would be lost.

## Your mastery check (private)
Can you explain why the substitute 403 does not depend on the handler?
