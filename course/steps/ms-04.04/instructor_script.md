# Instructor script — Conflict merge

1. (3 min) Ask: two phones edit one list offline; what must be true after sync? Collect answers.
2. (5 min) Name the three laws and give one tiny example of each.
3. (7 min) Walk through cmpDoc, the status rank and the hubFields lines in merge.ts.
4. (5 min) Show the faulty element-rank scenario; have learners predict each order.
5. (5 min) Run packages/core/test/merge.test.mjs and show how a seed replays a failure.
6. (5 min) Check yourself questions aloud.
